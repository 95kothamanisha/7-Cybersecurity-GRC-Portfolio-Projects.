# Risk Assessment — Management Summary
**Method:** likelihood 1–5 multiplied by impact 1–5. Local project rating thresholds: 1–4 Low; 5–9 Medium; 10–14 High; 15–25 Critical. These thresholds are **project assumptions**, not universal ISO thresholds.

## Top three (simulated)
1. **R-001: Customer database unauthorized access (20)** — review privileges, implement least privilege and recurring approvals.
2. **R-004: Microsoft 365 credential theft (20)** — verify MFA and improve phishing resistance.
3. **R-003: Endpoint malware (16)** — enforce patching, EDR coverage and awareness.

## Follow-up
Confirm assumptions with asset owners, inspect control evidence, assign dates, and score residual risk **after** treatment effectiveness is validated. Residual risk is intentionally marked for reassessment.
