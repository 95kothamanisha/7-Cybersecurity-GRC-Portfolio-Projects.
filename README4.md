# Incident Response Plan and Tabletop
**Portfolio case study:** NovaCloud AI Solutions (fictional). All records and findings are simulated; no real certification, vendor audit, AWS verification or external assurance is claimed.

## Project workflow
Detect → triage → contain → eradicate → recover → lessons learned.

## Files
- `Incident_Response_Plan.md`

## How I built it
I defined a fictional SaaS environment, selected relevant risks and controls, created structured workbooks and reports, assigned owners and follow-up actions, and explicitly separated assumptions from validated evidence.

## How to explain it in an interview
Walk through the fictional phishing timeline, identify owners, and explain evidence preservation.
