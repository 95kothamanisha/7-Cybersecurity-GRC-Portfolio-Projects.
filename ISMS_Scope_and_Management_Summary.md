# ISMS Scope and Management Summary
**Organization:** NovaCloud AI Solutions (fictional, 50-person AI SaaS provider). **Assessment:** simulated learning exercise, not certification or an actual audit.

## Scope
Cloud-hosted AI document-processing service, customer information, employee endpoints, identity and access, software development, critical SaaS suppliers, backups and security operations. AWS, Microsoft 365 and GitHub are in scope. Physical datacenter operations performed by AWS are supplier-managed and assessed through supplier assurance, not directly operated by NovaCloud.

## Objective and approach
Inventory assets; identify risk; select relevant ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls; record owner, status, evidence expected, gaps and improvement action. The workbook assesses a **selected subset** of controls, not all 93 and not ISO clauses 4–10 in full.

## Findings
Priority improvements: access recertification, supplier due diligence, restore testing, vulnerability remediation SLAs and centralized monitoring. Evidence entries are examples to request, not actual collected evidence.

## Next steps
Approve policy; validate status with owners; request and inspect evidence; assign dates; track remediation; review residual risk and prepare a Statement of Applicability before claiming implementation readiness.
