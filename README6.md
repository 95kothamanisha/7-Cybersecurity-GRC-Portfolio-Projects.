# GRC Compliance Dashboard
**Portfolio case study:** NovaCloud AI Solutions (fictional). All records and findings are simulated; no real certification, vendor audit, AWS verification or external assurance is claimed.

## Project workflow
Raw control/risk/action data → formulas → management metrics.

## Files
- `Dashboard_Interpretation.md`
- `GRC_Compliance_Dashboard.xlsx`

## How I built it
I defined a fictional SaaS environment, selected relevant risks and controls, created structured workbooks and reports, assigned owners and follow-up actions, and explicitly separated assumptions from validated evidence.

## How to explain it in an interview
Change one control status and see the dashboard update; explain why status counts are not a compliance certification.

## Your hands-on task before publishing
Review the workbook, explain one example without reading notes, change a sample value, and document what changed. Replace placeholders with genuine redacted evidence only after you perform the work yourself.
