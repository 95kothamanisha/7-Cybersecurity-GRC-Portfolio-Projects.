# Mock Internal Audit Report — NovaCloud
**Assessment type:** portfolio simulation / self-review. **Criteria:** 10 selected ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls. **No real company evidence was inspected.**

## Executive summary
The simulated review found recurring documentation and assurance gaps in access recertification, backup restore testing, supplier due diligence and vulnerability remediation. Findings are **illustrative**, not actual verified nonconformities. Formal severity would require criteria, objective evidence, context and auditor judgment.

## Objective, scope and methodology
Objective: test whether documented controls are defined, evidenced and assigned. Scope: selected governance, people and technology controls. Method: inspect fictional baseline assumptions against expected evidence and record follow-up requests. No interviews, production tests or independent validation were performed.

## Findings and recommendations
- **Access reviews (AUD-003):** simulated missing quarterly sign-offs. Recommend owner-approved review and proof of revocation.
- **Backup restore (AUD-009):** no simulated restore-test record. Recommend recovery exercise and recorded result.
- **Supplier assurance (AUD-004):** incomplete assessment. Recommend risk-ranked review.
- **Vulnerability management (AUD-008):** remediation SLA not documented. Recommend SLA and tracked exceptions.

## Corrective-action verification
Require owner, deadline, root-cause validation, remediation evidence and retest before closing. A self-review cannot claim independent ISO 27001 internal-audit conformance.

## Lessons learned
Evidence quality matters as much as written policy. Traceability between assets, risks, controls, gaps and action owners improves audit readiness.
